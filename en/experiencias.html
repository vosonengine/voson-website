Some of our experiences

A demo? A cultural installation? A fair that wants to be something more?

We create VR experiences for those who are not content with just telling things: they want the public to live them.

Goya

An immersive gallery subsidized by the Ministry of Culture. Up to 20 people explore the life and work of Goya, from his workshop to his darkest stages.

Frida Kahlo

Designed for Artika Books, this experience turns their book "The Dreams of Frida Kahlo" into a living work, as if you were entering the book itself.

Plaza del Obispo

We turned this emblematic square for the Malaga city council into a virtual stage where heritage comes to life.

MetaPolo

A sample of what virtual reality and free roam can do when you want to impress at an event fair.

VR Meditation

A wellness experience translated into several languages. Guided, gentle, and deep, for those who want a moment of real pause.

MetaCardio

Designed for Daiichi Sankyo. An educational experience to explain cardiology concepts in an immersive and clear way.

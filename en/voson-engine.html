In 2021

A client called us in desperation. He had a booth at a fair and what was supposed to be a "powerful activation" was turning into a very boring, very simple VR experience with some 360º photos.

He asked us for something different. Something that would make people stop. We created a five-minute VR experience. You put on the glasses and entered a cooperative experience with other people. People, lots of interactions, and lots of laughs. And when it was over, we gave them a QR code.

20-minute queues. Photos. Videos. And most importantly: leads. We had to buy more headsets because at that time they were burning from so much use. 400 real contacts. And a comment that sums it all up:

"This has been the most original experience of the entire fair."

That's what we do. That's how we were born. We don't make pretty videos. Our client is still remembered for that experience he brought. That's what we do. Experiences that don't end when the glasses come off. Experiences that are not forgotten.
